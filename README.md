# 📊 Superstore Sales Performance Analysis

## 🎯 Project Objective

The goal of this project was to analyze retail sales data to identify key growth drivers and profitability trends. I transformed raw business data into actionable insights to help understand which categories and regions are performing best.

## 🛠️ Technical Workflow

* **Data Cleaning (Excel):** Standardized date formats and performed initial data validation to ensure accuracy.
* **Exploratory Data Analysis (SQL):** Developed complex queries to extract key performance indicators (KPIs) such as Year-over-Year (YoY) profit growth and category-wise margins.
* **Data Visualization (Power BI):** Designed an interactive dashboard to communicate complex data through intuitive visuals and trend lines.

## 💡 Strategic Insights

* **Profitability Leadership:** **Technology** emerged as the primary profit driver, significantly outperforming Furniture and Office Supplies.
* **Growth Trajectory:** The business demonstrates a healthy upward trend, with **2017** showing peak performance in both sales and profit.
* **Market Dominance:** The **West Region** and the **Consumer Segment** represent the largest portion of the business, contributing to over 50% of total revenue.
